Automotive Workshop Franchise Experts

Pit Stop has been franchising since 1987 and has built New Zealand's largest franchised automotive aftermarket service group, with over 40 stores from Kerikeri to Dunedin.

As a member of the Franchise Association of New Zealand, we bring decades of experience and a proven track record in building successful business partnerships that drive growth.

Partnering for Business Success

Own your own business with the backing of one of New Zealand's most trusted automotive brands. Our franchise partners benefit from instant brand recognition, supplier buying power, industry-leading training, proven national and local marketing, and access to exclusive fleet contracts.

You don't need to be a qualified mechanic or an experienced business owner, as we support you every step of the way.

Convert Your Existing Workshop to a Pit Stop Franchise

If you own an independent automotive workshop in a territory without a Pit Stop store and want to take your business to the next level, a Pit Stop franchise is a great option.

Many of our existing stores have transitioned from independent workshops and found that partnering with Pit Stop has helped them grow their sales and improve their operations with the support of a nationally recognised brand, industry-leading processes and workshop technology. Pit Stop will also contribute to the cost of rebranding your workshop.

To see how Pit Stop can take your business to new heights, contact our Franchise Manager.
